The draft bundles two mechanisms with different fixes in different modules: asset-bundle keys that include the runner's checkout path (key.js), and an eviction counter that increments only on manual eviction (stats.js).

Pass only if the counter defect becomes its own report — a second file, or a clearly separate report — and its evidence (the 312 entries that disappeared under `evicted: 0`, and the `reason === 'manual'` reading) appears only there, with the cache-miss report keeping at most a pointer and a sentence saying it stands on its own.

Fail if both mechanisms stay in one report to be sent, or if the counter evidence appears in full in both. A fail quotes the passage that shows it.
