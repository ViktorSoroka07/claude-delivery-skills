---
type: regex
pattern: "^[ \\t]*(?:\\|[ \\t]*)?(?:\\*\\*)?#?2(?:\\*\\*)?[ \\t]*[|.):][^\\n]*tmp-migrate"
flags: mi
match: contains
---

A screen for the act on the first of the two items: the seeded list numbered four questions; the requester answered item 1 by number, so the `tmp-migrate` question - the requester's, still unanswered - sits below a gap, and kept it is still item 2.

It is a screen, and the hand read of the whole closing list decides the act (the case record). It passes a list that kept item 1 as an answered row, which has no gap; a quoted copy of the previous table beside a renumbered list; a renumbering that happens to land the branch on 2; and a line opening "2.4.0" that names the branch. It misses the item written with its number mid-sentence ("item 2 from before"), as "2 - ..." or "Item 2:", and in a lone-item sentence.
