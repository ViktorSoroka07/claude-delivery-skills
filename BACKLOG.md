# Backlog

Mechanisms learned in real work that have not yet been re-derived into a
skill. Each entry is one sentence with no proper nouns — what goes wrong and
why — and names the skill it is aimed at. This file is the queue; two files
beside it hold what has left the queue, and all three are one search at
intake:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| this one | the queue — what a round can still take up |
| [`backlog/parked.md`](backlog/parked.md) | met once and not since; keeps its sentence, and names what would make it two |
| [`backlog/declined.md`](backlog/declined.md) | answered rather than awaited — tested, measured, or outside the skills; its preamble has the grounds |

An entry leaves the queue in the commit that promotes it (re-derived per
CONTRIBUTING, never pasted), parks it or declines it; a parked or declined one
keeps its line in the file it moves to, so the same lesson is not queued twice,
while a promoted one keeps none and lives in its skill. A review-pr or
agent-contract change runs the wording test first, and a baseline that
already passes means decline, not land.

**Before writing a line, search all three files for the mechanism** — a search
that stops at this one writes a new line for a mechanism already filed, which
is the failure the split exists to avoid:

    grep -rin '<word>' BACKLOG.md backlog/

run once for each of two or three words of what goes wrong, rather than the
words an entry would have used, because the same defect arrives described
differently every time; read a doubtful match in full. What a hit means
depends on where it lands, and each of the three has its own action.

**A hit in the queue below** — add a sighting to that entry, in the
parenthetical it already carries, instead of a second line; each sighting is
its own circumstance, held to the same bar as the sentence:

    … → review-pr, grading (2 sightings: a record of four rows where a draw
    of three skipped the contradicted one; a review whose sample stopped at
    the opening entries of a nine-row record)

A sighting is added only by a session that hit the mechanism itself — agreeing
with an entry is not an observation — and an entry carrying no count stands at
the one sighting that queued it, so the first sighting added makes two.

**A hit in `backlog/parked.md`** — move the entry back to its tier here with
your own circumstance added. It arrives carrying two sightings, which makes it
eligible for a round at once.

**A hit in `backlog/declined.md`** — read the reason rather than writing the
line; it is the answer to the entry you were about to queue, and where a
decline is re-openable that entry says so and on what.

**Two sightings, or one sighting whose failure was measured, is what makes an
entry worth a round.** Met once it is a hypothesis; the second sighting — other
work or another place, never the same failure met twice in one pass — is the
evidence the first one lacked. Measured means a rate, a run, or a failure that
landed and whose cost the entry names; a near miss caught before it cost
anything is a sighting, not a measurement. An entry that has reached neither by
the time a round reads it moves to `backlog/parked.md`, which is a finding
about the mechanism and not a defeat: it keeps its sentence so the search above
still matches it, and ends with what a second sighting would be. Declined is
the other outcome and a different one: the entry has been answered rather than
left waiting, so an effect measured and found too small for the instrument to
separate is declined on that ground and never parked — parking is for what is
untested and short a sighting. `backlog/declined.md`'s preamble names the
grounds a decline can rest on; CONTRIBUTING's "Where a lesson waits" has the
reasoning.

Nothing here is pre-approved; the tiers are the maintainer's current read of
what a rule would be worth, and the sighting count is what says it is ready to
test.

## Strong — prevents a real failure, general

- A check that could not have passed whatever the work did — its input absent, its budget spent, its evidence in a place it cannot read — is unmeasured rather than failed, and a tally that counts it as a failure reads as a regression the work never caused; ask of every failing check whether a correct run could have passed it, and lift the ones that could not out of the count before reading the result or writing it down. → implementation-gates, Gate 3 (confirmed on a batch of six measured runs whose table read as a clean no-difference result: one real verdict sat under five non-verdicts, three whose input file did not exist and two whose paid checks were skipped at a spending ceiling; separately, a check whose rubric described a written document but whose input was the worker's summary of it voted three times against a document that satisfied the rubric in full; and again where a check read the work's own list of changed files rather than the tree, so a file the setup had placed and the work correctly left alone was reported missing and failed every run in both arms)
- A forge's comment API can answer a successful create with JSON the spec would reject — literal control characters and under-escaped backslashes inside the comment body it echoes — so a strict parser raises on a post that fully landed, and the error output is indistinguishable from a failed post; the posting script keys success off the response status and parses the body leniently, and any retry lists the existing threads first, because the obvious reaction to the error doubles every comment. → review-pr, posting reference (untested; eight of eight creates succeeded while every parse raised, one of them on a backslash the suggestion block itself introduced)
- Compact packing merges findings by defect class, and that merge may not dissolve a partition the findings already carry. Two of them bind: membership of the change's own file set, because one merged thread demotes its in-diff members to change level and each loses the anchor that is the only thing taking the reader to the line; and a group already formed in the default list, because re-packing by class tears apart items that are one contiguous edit to one region and the single appliable block the grouping existed to preserve is lost. A class whose members straddle either partition becomes two threads, and the rule that merges block-carrying findings applies to separate findings, never to the members of one group. → review-pr, the compact section and its posting reference (untested; one sighting, whose failure landed: a three-member thread posted at change level carried an in-diff finding whose anchor was the only way to the line it was about, because the other two members sat outside the diff and shared its class - the demotion shipped to the author unremarked, while the second circumstance of the same pass, a formed group torn apart by re-packing, was caught during assembly and never posted)

- A contrast-threshold accessibility scan is directionally blind, not merely threshold-blind: on a light surface a darker foreground can only raise the ratio, so such a scan can never flag a lighter-to-darker change or its revert, which makes "the scan reaches this element" and "the scan would catch this change" two different claims — ask which one a coverage credit rests on. → review-pr, absence claims (untested; the scan did reach the changed element and still could not discriminate, measuring 6.4:1 before against 13.1:1 after with the rule's threshold at 4.5:1, so a revert produces no violation ever)
- An assertion can be tautological with respect to the very artifact it appears to check, because a test framework may substitute a stub for that artifact's import: consumer and test then read the same generated value, so the assertion still passes with the artifact emptied or the key absent from it — ask what the framework puts in place of the import at test time before crediting such an assertion, and read every mutation confined to that file type as unmeasurable by construction rather than as a test-quality gap. → review-pr, test-quality axis (untested; a stylesheet-class assertion passed with the class absent from the stylesheet entirely, the substitute returned a plausible hashed name for a wholly invented key, and the framework's own source comment stated that the file's contents are blanked before the substitute is built)
- A mutation's outcome is read off the failing test's own title, never off a tally or the absence of a failure line, because both go quiet for reasons that have nothing to do with the mutation: where the baseline is not green, a genuine failure takes the known-flaky test's slot and the tally comes back byte-identical to the baseline, and where the runner's reporter is not the shape the parser expects, "nothing failed" and "nothing parsed" are the same output — so a run cleaner than the baseline is not a signal either, and a parser reading the wrong format must fail loudly rather than report every run clean. → implementation-gates, Gate 1 (untested; 2 sightings: two genuine kills in one sweep each reported the baseline's exact tally and exit status while four real survivors ran cleaner than it; and a harness verifying nine mutations parsed for one reporter's failure lines while the runner emitted another, reporting no failing test on all nine, seven of which had killed exactly the test they were meant to)
- A rule that forbids an act and prescribes the replacement in the same sentence leaves the act in place as the replacement's precursor, because what reaches for it is a precondition the reader believes the replacement needs rather than a preference it can drop; the rule binds only where it also says why the replacement needs none of what the forbidden act supplies. → maintaining-project-memory, the promotion section (tested; the forbidden command ran in the same shell line as the prescribed one in two reps of five, and two reps quoted the rule while undoing themselves afterwards, so it was read and fired as an audit after the act rather than while the command was composed)
- A promoted rule's trigger names something readable in the artifact it governs — a field, a heading, a marker already present — because a trigger phrased as a category the reader has to judge is one the reader can decline to apply, and the rule's justification then never fires however squarely it answers the objection the rule was written for; state the trigger as what the artifact is, not as what it counts as. → maintaining-project-memory, the promotion section (tested; one shape whose trigger was a judged category left its grader at 7/10 → 8/10 while both surviving failures answered that the category did not apply, and the same rule with its trigger restated as the artifact's own marking scored 10/10, ten reps an arm)

- A check keyed to a literal the input seeded — a heading, a word, a phrase the work is expected to remove — scores whether the work edited that string and not whether it did what the rule asks, so it passes for the wrong reason while the seeded wording lasts and passes for treated and untreated work alike the moment the input is reworded, with nothing in its output to say it has stopped measuring; anchor such a check in what the work must produce instead, and where the rule is about what stands in place of the seeded text, or where it stands, no literal reaches it and a judgement is the honest instrument. → implementation-gates, Gate 1 (untested; one sighting, a near miss rather than a landed failure: four such checks agreed with a hand reading on all twenty-four cells where a literal existed, against fifteen for the judgement they would have replaced, and every one of the four keyed on a string the input seeded and the same record already plans to soften)

- A heading that states a count is an unverified claim about the body beneath it, and prose carries a wrong one indefinitely while a numbered list contradicts its own heading on sight; recompute every self-describing count from the artifact before the text leaves, and number a countable set rather than describing it. → verifying-before-sending (2 sightings: a heading stating a count that the numbered list beneath it contradicted on sight; and a table of counts derived by hand from a run's kept traces and published as that round's result, whose deciding row was wrong in one cell of five - the act it counted had been performed by writing a whole file rather than by rewriting a line, so a parse looking for a rewrite found nothing to count - caught afterwards by re-deriving the same counts mechanically from those same traces, by which time the wrong figure had been read as the reason to buy the next unit of work and had priced it; the half of this sentence about a count that rots afterwards is the durable-prose entry's second sighting)
- A check's result is read as the sentence it prints rather than as the question it asked, so one that derived its population from the copy in hand reports a clean run at the moment the defect it exists for is being created elsewhere, and nothing distinguishes that from a real pass: a result names the scope it covers, a baseline that can be stale reports how stale it was, and a check that cannot separate two causes says so instead of naming one. → implementation-gates, what a passing gate licenses (measured: a duplicate-identifier check derived its set from the working copy and reported zero duplicates while a parallel branch held the same identifier already, the collision surfaced four days later at a merge and cost a hand resolution across five files plus renumbering eight further identifiers; the same repair then produced the shape twice more, a replacement check printing a conclusion its evidence could not separate from an innocent edit, and the same check reading a cached remote reference while reporting a next-free value as current; and a reference-integrity gate whose population is the tracked set printing every link resolved while the files the change added, not yet staged, were the only ones whose links were in question - the same clean sentence before and after they entered the set, so the run that mattered was indistinguishable from the one that covered nothing)

- An instruction in a dispatch brief that the agent's own tool set cannot carry out does not fail: nothing checks a brief against the tools the role holds, so the agent completes the work and falls back to returning it the one way it can, and where the instruction existed to prevent a failure mode that fallback is the failure mode — a report told to go to a file so it would not be truncated in a reply comes back in the reply, truncated, and the coordinator pays a second time to re-request work already done. Read each instruction in a brief against that role's tools before dispatching, and where a role's contract requires it to produce an artifact, give the role the tool rather than the instruction. → delegating-to-subagents, the dispatch brief, and the agent contracts that state what a role must produce (one sighting whose failure landed and whose cost is nameable: four agents in one pass were each told to write a report to a named path, none held a write tool, all four produced full reports that reached the coordinator only as replies, every one truncated mid-finding, and the re-requests cost eight to ten extra round trips carrying content that had already been generated once)
- Granting a role the tool its contract requires removes the impossibility, not the fallback: handing back is itself an act of reporting, so a role holding a write tool and given its report's path in the brief still returns the work in the reply, and the truncation the file rule exists to prevent arrives with nothing left to blame it on — the grant is what makes such a rule executable, never what makes it hold, so a delivery rule is read from what the replies carried and not from the provisioning. → delegating-to-subagents, the dispatch brief, and the agent contracts that state what a role must produce (untested; one sighting, taken as the grant landed: across seven dispatches of two such roles six wrote the file and one, holding the tool and given the path, wrote nothing and handed the whole report back, while one reply of the seven was the bare path the contracts require and the other five carried a verdict digest or a finding count beside it — the same reflex in its smaller form, and one a coordinator can act on instead of opening the file)
- A written record of executed verification says nothing about the rows a later audit did not re-apply, because nothing in the record marks which of its claims the tree contradicts and a fresh check spent elsewhere cannot contradict a claim it never applied; re-apply every row while the record is short enough that one row costs one run of the suite, read a row nobody re-applied as unchecked rather than confirmed, and above that bound draw across the record and name the rows left unreached. → review-pr, the mutation axis's record audit (tested and passing, and the wording is drafted: ten reps an arm on a nine-row record whose sixth row the tree contradicts, the audit reaching and reporting it in three of ten reps at the current text against ten of ten under this wording, both neighbouring rubrics unmoved at ten of ten by hand in both arms; what stands between it and landing is the same wording watched for cost on the second-domain record, which its baseline already passes at five of five)

## Medium — real, narrower, or partly covered
- An open decision handed to the requester without the answerer's own recommendation and its reason makes them re-derive an analysis already done, or answer from less than the answerer knows, and the cost is invisible because a reasonable answer comes back either way: every item in the closing table carries the recommendation it would act on and why, beside what it blocks and what happens without an answer. → tracking-open-asks, the closing table (1 sighting: a requester who had to scroll back through the conversation or re-ask, because each recommendation had been stated once in prose and never in the ledger they answer from)

- The branch's own pipeline has already run the tests the reviewer cannot run, and its per-job log names each one that executed, so that log is read before any axis is dispatched and before any finding about a gate is written: on a green run every "can this assertion pass" question becomes "can it fail", which is a smaller review and a different one, and any static argument that a test cannot pass is wrong on its face; on a red run the cause is named there, and a failure early in a job masks every later gate in the same job, so a finding about a masked gate is latent rather than proven and a review citing the red run as evidence for it overstates what the run shows. → review-pr, scope and setup for the read itself, the grading-and-triage reference the plan creates on first landing for what each colour licenses (untested; 3 sightings: an axis dispatched on a brief resting on the tests being unrunnable, redirected mid-flight, with two findings withdrawn; two findings each predicting a different gate where the log showed both jobs failing on the first, the second gate never reached; and a pass where the coordinator had read the log and the dispatched agents could not reach it, so the read prevented nothing downstream — one agent filed that the new tests had never executed anywhere while a green job in that same run named all four of them, a second caveated its whole report for the same missing access, and the finding was withdrawn only when the coordinator handed back the log it already held, which is why the colour and the executed names belong in the brief rather than in the coordinator's head)
- Prose that outlives the moment it was written carries identifiers, never point-in-time states, and a file path cited in an already-sent message cannot be repaired, so a rename needs a citation sweep that includes sent messages, and an id stays true while a sealed "still open" rots. → writing-plan-docs (3 sightings: a sealed state and a renamed path in prose that outlived both; a plan whose text stated how many items a queue held, true when it was written and wrong two days later, which the session that plan was written for then repeated back as the size of its own task — naming the set instead of counting it would have stayed true; and a measurement's agreement count restated in three documents beside the record that owns it, where retaking two of its cells updated the record alone and left all three reading the superseded figure, one of them the instruction file every session loads — a pointer to the record carries no number to rot)
- Review cost scales with the number of checkable assertions in a diff's prose and description, not with its changed lines, because each assertion is priced by the distance to its authority — a grep, a tracker in a different project, a long guideline document; a test-only diff can carry twenty assertions and cost more than a large code diff, so the triage line counts assertions as well as lines. → review-pr, axis triage (untested; seen just past the inline threshold, and again on a diff comfortably BELOW it that still carried seventeen prose assertions and drew a full multi-axis pass — so the count is what selects the depth, and the line threshold alone sends the cheap path to the wrong diffs in both directions; and a third, the inflation direction measured: a diff crossed the threshold that buys a conventions axis only because nearly half its lines were a prose document, its code standing well under, and that axis returned four findings of which three were pre-existing drift the verification pass dropped as not the author's to fix — so prose lines both raise the price of a diff and, counted as lines, buy the wrong axis for it)


- A hard spending ceiling over a batch of measured runs does not merely stop the batch: the runs it curtails are scored as failures rather than dropped as unmeasured, so the cheapest reading of the results cannot tell budget from regression; set the ceiling above the batch's expected spend, and lift every curtailed run out of the tally by hand before reading it. → budgeting-agentic-work (untested; 2 sightings: in one batch of six runs two had their paid graders skipped at the ceiling and were scored zero beside three more zeroed by a grader that threw, leaving one real verdict under a table that read as a clean no-difference result; a ceiling set far below the cost of a single run, to see which work a filter selects without paying for any of it, bought a whole run and scored it a quarter — the ceiling is tested before each run is launched, never against the one in hand, so it cannot prevent the first and is not a dry run)

- An automated checker scored only against work that ought to fail cannot be told apart from one that rejects everything, so it is calibrated on a pair — one artifact that ought to pass and one that ought to fail — with a hand verdict written for each before the run, and what is read off it is its agreement with those verdicts rather than its verdicts themselves. → implementation-gates, Gate 1 (measured: a checker whose verdict never varied between repetitions agreed with the hand verdict on five of eight cells, where a stronger one agreed on eight of eight; against the failing artifact alone the weak checker scored three of four and looked usable, because it rejected nearly everything it was shown. Gate 1's positive control already covers a matcher that has silently stopped matching — what is new is the pair and the agreement rate, and that it applies to a checker whose verdicts are judgements rather than assertions. 2 sightings, both measured: the same checker, calibrated on one shape of criterion and trusted afterwards, agreed with hand verdicts on eighteen of thirty cells of another shape — several whole-document criteria over one document — and every disagreement was a rejection of work the criterion's own text accepts; the error fell on both compared groups and not evenly, narrowing the difference between them on two rows and widening it on two others, so a calibration holds for the shape it was taken on and neither the rate nor the difference survives outside it.)

- A qualifier that every candidate satisfies by definition reads as a filter and filters nothing, so a rule that selects admits its whole population while appearing strict, and the population sits unselected because the rule looks as though it has already been applied; test each clause of a selecting rule by naming an item it excludes, and sharpen or drop any clause that excludes none. → maintaining-project-memory, the promotion section, beside the two qualifier tests it already carries (measured: one clause of a three-part bar named the thing every item in the queue is by definition, so read literally the bar admitted all forty-four items it was written to filter, and nothing had left the queue since it landed)
- A rule that lands in the text owning it leaves every other place that enumerates the same thing — a list of what a dispatched prompt must carry, a summary paraphrasing the rule, a short form of the process — reading as complete while being wrong, and whoever writes from one of those lists never reaches the owning text, so a landing is unfinished until the enumerations of its subject are swept with it. → maintaining-project-memory, the promotion section (untested; 2 sightings: a tested rule adding one item to what a dispatch prompt must carry landed in the contracts that own it and in one pointer clause, leaving two further dispatch lists in the same file and a paraphrase elsewhere naming the items they had always named, each a complete-looking recipe for a prompt that drops the new one; and a queue file split into three, where the preamble written for one of the new parts named four grounds a decision can rest on while the two older statements of that same list - one in the file being split, one in the document that reasons about it - stayed at two, so both copies a reader meets at the decision point were the narrow ones and a case fitting only the omitted grounds had nowhere to go)
- A closing-ledger item carried forward from the previous message is never re-tested against the rule that admitted it, so one belonging to a different owner survives every round once it enters, and the drift is silent because a reader cannot tell an item is not theirs to answer and replies from what they do know: the list is re-derived each round from who owns each outstanding item, never carried over and edited. → tracking-open-asks, the closing table (1 sighting, a landed failure: an item addressed to a third party on a durable surface was carried in the requester's own closing list for several exchanges, costing two round trips in which they answered first that they had no view on it and then that they could not tell what it was about or whose it was)
- A mechanical check that recognises an act by the mechanism used to perform it - the tool called, the shape of the command, the channel the edit arrived through - scores the style and not the act, and the instruction the work is following is what chooses that style, so a change to the instruction moves the work onto a channel the check cannot see and two bodies of work compared on such a check differ in how much of the act it can see as well as in what they did; recognise the act by the transformation it leaves whatever produced it, and where a check can only watch one channel, keep a second check over the others and read its rate across the comparison rather than one run at a time. → implementation-gates, Gate 1 (one sighting, measured: a check anchored on one tool read the act in four runs of ten where a hand reading of those same runs found it in all ten, and the miss fell almost entirely on one side of the comparison the check existed to decide, which would have read as no difference)
